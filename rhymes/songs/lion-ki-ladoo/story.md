# Lion Ki Ladoo: what this rhyme means

## In plain words

The rhyme is about a lion cub named Sheru who loves eating colorful sweets. The song celebrates joy and fun as the lion dances and plays with others in the jungle, making everyone happy.

**Lesson:** It's important to enjoy life and share happiness with friends.

## Characters

- **Sheru** the lion (main character): called Sheru in the song
