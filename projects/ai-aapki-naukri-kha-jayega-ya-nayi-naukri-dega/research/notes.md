- 19 September 2026  
- 56% global manufacturing executives use AI agents  
- 37% deploy over ten AI tools (per Google Cloud–National Research Group survey)  
- Deployment–impact gap exists: installing AI software alone doesn’t raise productivity

- India had approximately 4.2 lakh AI professionals in 2024

- India needs workforce upskilling, indigenous data infrastructure, ethical safeguards for AI readiness  
- Measures include skilling workers, redesigning workflows, worker participation, and continuous learning

## Source: AI as a Tool for Augmentation Not Automation - LinkedIn
https://www.linkedin.com/top-content/technology/artificial-intelligence-in-business/ai-as-a-tool-for-augmentation-not-automation/

- AI ka Indian job market par impact: software developers, customer support, designers, teachers aur students par padega  
- Automation vs augmentation: AI ko co-pilot banaye jaa sakta hai, jo human thought ko extend kare  
- New AI careers: AI mentors, AI-powered learning platforms, AI coaching tools ke field mein naya career options hain  
- Skills to learn: critical thinking, cognitive hygiene, active reasoning, verification aur questioning skills zaroori hain  
- Realistic future scenarios: AI-enabled teams performance human-only teams se aage chalte hain, synergy ki wajah se maximum benefit milega  
- Official advice: transparency through internal logs, subliminal techniques banane par band, neurorights ke liye cognitive privacy protect karna  
- Examples: JA Boost platform me AI mentors develop kar rahe hain, customer support me chatbots use hota hai, designers me AI tools design process ko assist karte hain  
- Cognitive diversity important hai: AI systems jo Western norms follow karte hain, local nuance ke expression ko kam karte hain  
- Education mein AI ka use: active reasoning exercises se critical thinking banta raha hai, AI outputs ko verify aur question karna zaroori hai

## Source: Automation vs. Augmentation: Will AI Replace or Enhance Jobs?
https://aifirstmindset.ai/blog/will-ai-replace-all-jobs/

- AI job augmentation is more common than full automation  
- AI handles data-heavy tasks like research, analysis, automation, and pattern identification  
- Human-AI synergy focuses on combining human judgment with AI insights  
- Few jobs are single-task; most involve routine and non-routine work together  
- AI replaces tasks within jobs, not entire roles  
- New skills needed include AI oversight, workflow design, data quality checks, prompt engineering, and human review  
- Upskilling for AI era is critical as job skills have changed twice as fast for AI-exposed roles  
- Realistic future shows little rise in unemployment despite AI impact  
- Junior roles saw hiring dips but overall employment remains stable  
- Companies should redesign work around human-AI collaboration for better productivity gains  
- Training should focus on evaluating AI outputs, not just using tools
