## Source: Impact of AI on India`s Job Market: Jobs, Skills & Careers
https://www.learnbay.co/blogs/impact-of-ai-on-indias-job-market

- Unemployment rate remains at 3.1% despite AI evolution  
- Over 290,000 new job postings were created in 2025 due to AI  
- IT job postings declined to 60,000+ in FY24 from 445,000 in FY22  
- AI is redefining roles rather than replacing them entirely  
- In 2026, AI usage in India is most concentrated in software (45.2%)  
- Net new tech jobs in FY24 increased by +60,000  
- Only ~1% of India’s workforce (~600M workers) is in AI-exposed IT roles  
- AI-focused hiring growth in India is +59.5% as of 2026  
- Over 43% of Indian workforce still works in agriculture, trade, construction, and manufacturing where AI cannot replace humans  
- About 5.8 million people are in AI-exposed IT and ITeS roles (1% of total labor force)  
- Only 16% of India’s total IT workforce is AI-skilled as of 2026  
- Over 62% of large-scale Indian firms use GenAI tools for tasks  
- Medium-sized enterprises are adopting AI faster than others  
- Generative AI and agentic models skills are crucial for IT professionals in 2026  
- AI literacy is more important than competing with AI
