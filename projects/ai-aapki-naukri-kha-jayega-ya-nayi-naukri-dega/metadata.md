## Description

AI ka impact Indian job market par kya hai? Software developers, customer support, designers aur students ke liye kya hoga? 2030 tak 30% jobs AI se khatam ho sakti hain. Iske baare mein jankari ke liye dekhie! AI ka matlab hota hai computer program jo likhna, bolna ya chehra pahchanna seekh leta hai. Agar aap ye nahi samajh paenge ki AI aapke kaam ko kaise badlega, to aap peeche rah jaenge aur aapki naukri khatre mein pad sakti hai. Otomeshan vs egzikyushan, new AI careers, skills to learn aur realistic future scenarios ke bare mein jankari. Aapko naye skils sikhne honge taki aapki naukri safe rahegi. 2024 mein 4.2 lakh AI propheshnals hai aur market USD 17 biliyan se zyada ka hai. Subscribe karo, janne mei apni naukri ki future!
