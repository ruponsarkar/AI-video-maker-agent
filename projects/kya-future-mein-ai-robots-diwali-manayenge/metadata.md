## Description

AI robots Bharat mein Diwali manane ki shuruaat kar sakte hain! Aapke ghar aur tyoharon ka anubhav badal jayega. Jaipur ki Gulmohar Garden Society mein humanoid robots ne diwali manai, sparkler jalae, logon se hath milae aur aagantukon ka svagat kiya. Robots na sirf tyoharon ko manane mein madad karenge balki rojmarra ki samasyaon ka samadhan bhi karenge. Aapke ghar mein pareshani ho jaye to ye robots turant use theek kar denge! AI tools se rangoli ban rahi hai, aarti ke bol suna sakta hai aur virtual tareeke se puja kar payenge. Robots suraksha nigrani, saphai aur rakhrakhav kaam bhi karte hain.
