## Source: Scammers using deepfake videos of PM, CMs in social media political ...
https://theprint.in/india/scammers-using-deepfake-videos-of-pm-cms-in-social-media-political-ads-to-dupe-people-i4c/3076267/

- I4C advisory October 2026: Deepfake ads on Facebook and Instagram impersonate PM, Finance Minister, and Chief Ministers  
- Fake welfare schemes promise financial assistance to the needy  
- Ads use manipulated videos and fake phone numbers for cashback/rewards via scratch cards or spin-and-win offers  
- Victims are directed to call +92 numbers (Pakistan’s country code) or fraudulent websites  
- Fraudsters trick victims into making repeated payments under false pretenses like taxes, fees, etc.  
- UPI PIN is used to transfer money, not to receive it  
- Victims are induced to enter their UPI PIN through fake payment apps or collect requests  
- NCTAU advises verifying welfare schemes on official government websites before acting on social media ads  
- Check the purpose and recipient of any fee/payment requested for a welfare benefit  
- Confirm identity of anyone claiming to represent a government department via official channels  
- Avoid sharing Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, OTPs, or UPI PINs with unknown persons  
- Verify videos/voice recordings of public officials through official sources  
- Always check transaction details before approving UPI collect requests or entering UPI PIN
