- India mein internet kahan se aata hai? Internet ke infrastructure ko submarine fiber optic cables, data centers, ISPs, mobile towers, routers aur satellites kehte hain.  
- India ka internet ecosystem sabse bada hai jo affordable mobile broadband aur digital infrastructure investment ki wajah se banaya gaya hai.  
- 2025 mein ITU Data Hub ke according, India mein 70% population internet user hai, jo 2018 ke 20.1% se zyada hai.

- India mein 31 operational IXPs hain, jo Asia Pacific region me sabse zyada hai.

- IXPs domestic traffic ko local exchange me le aate hain, latency kam hoti hai aur transit cost ghat jata hai.  
- Internet infrastructure ke liye private sector investment aur spectrum deployment important hai.  
- Digital literacy aur infrastructure quality mein challenges baqi hain, jo digital economy ka growth karte hue continue ho raha hai.

## Source: Is India's internet boom running into an undersea problem?
https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx2r2mzelxko

- India's internet relies on submarine fiber optic cables that land near Mumbai, Chennai, Cochin, Tuticorin, and Trivandrum.  
- Around Versova in Mumbai, 13 of India's 18 international cables come ashore within 6 km, carrying 95% of its westward bandwidth to Europe, Africa, and West Asia.  
- These cables are the internet's hidden highways, carrying about 99% of global communication traffic over 870,000 miles (1.4 million km) of ocean floor.  
- India has only 3% of the world’s submarine cables but accounts for 20% of global data consumption.  
- India has 21 cable landing stations, which is about 1% of the world's total.  
- 11 of India's 18 international cables are over 20 years old and nearing their 25-year lifespan.  
- Cable faults are a common risk, with an estimated 150–200 faults happening globally each year.  
- Data can be rerouted through other landing points like Chennai or Kochi if some cables are cut.  
- India's growing digital economy depends heavily on this invisible infrastructure but has limited control over it.  
- Experts say India needs to increase its cable landing stations several times to support its digital growth.

## Source: Subsea Cable Network & Global Fibre Network Explained
https://www.airtel.in/b2b/insights/blogs/subsea-cable-network-global-fibre-network-explained

- Internet ka infrastructure subsea fiber optic cables se start hota hai jo samundar ke neechey chhupi hain  
- 99% intercontinental internet traffic subsea cables se ho jata hai, satellites nahi  
- 600 active aur planned submarine cables 1.4 million km ocean floor par spread hain  
- India mein internet pahunchta hai through subsea cables jo data centers, ISPs, mobile towers, routers aur satellites ke saath connect hote hain  
- Subsea cables ki speed light ke saamne hoti hai, data milliseconds me travel karta hai  
- 500 Tbps capacity wale cables me Wavelength Division Multiplexing (WDM) technology use hoti hai jo hundreds of different colours me light ko split karta hai  
- Ek single fibre me 200,000 HD video calls, 50 million emails, 10 million webpage requests simultaneously transmit ho sakte hain  
- Google ke Dunant cable me 250 Tbps capacity hai jo 31,250 full-length movies per second download kar sakta hai  
- 2Africa cable 37,000 km long hai aur 180 Tbps capacity wala hai jo 33 countries ko connect karta hai  
- Cable Landing Stations (CLS) subsea cables ke ocean se land tak connection ka junction hota hai  
- CLS me Branching Units, Power Feed Equipment, Signal Regenerators aur Monitoring Systems shamil hote hain  
- Monitoring Systems Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) use karte hain jo 1 km resolution wala hai  
- Indian businesses international markets ke saath connect hone ke liye CLS se global fibre networks tak data flow hota hai
