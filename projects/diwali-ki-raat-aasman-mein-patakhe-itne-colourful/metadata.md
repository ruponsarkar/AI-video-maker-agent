## Description

Fireworks ke rang ka rahasya kya hai? Diwali ki raat mein aasman me itna colourful dikhta hai kyunki data lavnon ke karan hote hain. Har dhatu alag-alag rang produce karti hai, jaise strontium lal aur barium hara. Atomic emission spectrum ka karana hai jo rangon ki tarah banata hai. Is video mein aap dekhenge ki patakhe itne rangin kyun dikhte hain aur yeh vijnan kaise kaam karta hai. Ab hume Delhi me pradushan ka samasya bhi batayenge aur green firecrackers ke istemal par Supreme Court ne kya karke dekha. Aapko pata chalta hai ki science se hamesha acha hota hai, to is video ko dekhke aapki baat banayein!
