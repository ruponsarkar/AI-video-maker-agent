## Description

Patakhe ke dhamake ka science, awaaz kaise banti hai? Jab patakh phutte, to hawa mein tezi se dabav ban jata hai aur isse shockwave uttarati hai. Aapne Diwali ki awaaz samajh li ho? Patakhe ke dhamake ke behan rahasya aapke liye yahan hai! Black powder ke jalne se gaisen tezi se phailti hain, jo hawa mein lahar banati hai. Ye lahar aapke kano tak pahunchkar awaz banti hai. Aapki janta hai ki patakhe ka dhamaka sirf ek dhakka nahi hai, balki science ke kamal hai! Sochenge ki gas ki tezi aur dabav se awaaz itni tez hoti hai? Patakhon ke dhamake ka rahasya aapke liye yahan hai. Diwali ke patakhe ke dhamake ka vijnan, ek baar dekho aur samajh lo!
