## Description

AI girlfriend ke zariye online pyar ke naam par lakhon ka fraud badh raha hai. Yeh computer program hai jo aapko accha mahsus karta hai, lekin yeh real pyar nahi hai. Is video mein aap dekhenge ki AI ka kaise dhokha hota hai, ek admi ne kathy naam ki AI girlfriend se baat ki, aur kuch log apne flirt practice ke liye iska istemal karte hain. Lekin yeh sab ek scam hai jo aapki jankari chura leke paise mangta hai. Aapko janta hai ki kisi ko paise na bhejen, agar koi aapko ajib lage to saibar help line ya police ko report karein. Online dating safety ke baare mein jankari milenge aur kaise dhokha nahi ho sake.
