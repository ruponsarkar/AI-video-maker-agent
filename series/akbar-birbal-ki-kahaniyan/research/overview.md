- The man succeeded by staring at a distant lamp to stay motivated.

- The moral is: Dishonesty always reveals itself in the end.

- One suspect hesitated, and Birbal declared him guilty.
- Akbar praised Birbal for his method.
- The moral is: The guilty often expose themselves through fear.
- Another story is "The Washerman’s Donkey".
- A dispute arose between a washerman and a nobleman over a donkey.
- Birbal dressed the donkey in elegant clothes and brought it to court.
- The nobleman mistook it for a valuable horse and claimed it.
- Akbar ruled in favour of the washerman.
- The moral is: Appearances can be misleading—it's important to dig deeper to uncover the truth.
