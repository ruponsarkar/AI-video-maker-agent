## Birbal Akbar Khichdi Khichri Story Information
https://www.scribd.com/doc/292637725/Birbal-Akbar-Khichdi-Khichri-Story-Information
- Akbar and Birbal take a walk along a lake on a cold winter day.  
- Birbal tells Akbar that a man would do anything for money.  
- Akbar doubts this and suggests no one would spend a night in the cold lake for money.  
- Birbal challenges Akbar to find someone who would do so and promises to reward them with a thousand gold coins.  
- Birbal finds a poor man desperate enough to accept the challenge.  
- The poor man enters the lake, and guards are posted to ensure he fulfills his promise.  
- The next morning, the guards take the poor man to Akbar.  
- Akbar asks if the poor man spent the night in the lake; he confirms it.  
- Akbar asks how the poor man survived the cold; the poor man says he focused on a street lamp nearby.  
- Akbar concludes the poor man used the warmth of the street lamp and decides not to reward him.  
- The poor man goes to Birbal for help.  
- The next day, Birbal does not go to court.  
- Akbar sends a messenger to Birbal's home.  
- The messenger returns saying Birbal will come only when his khichri is cooked.  
- Akbar waits but Birbal does not arrive.  
- Akbar goes to Birbal's house and finds him sitting near burning twigs with a bowl of khichri hanging five feet above the fire.  
- Akbar and his attendants laugh at the situation.  
- Akbar asks how the khichri can be cooked if it is so far from the fire.  
- Birbal explains that the poor man received heat from a street lamp more than a furlong away, implying the khichri will cook by the same principle.  
- Akbar realizes his mistake and decides to reward the poor man.
