# THE TRUE MERCHANT TEST

## Akbar Birbal Ki Kahaniyan | अकबर और बीरबल ...
https://www.tathyatarang.com/article/short-stories/akbar-birbal-short-story-with-moral
- Akbar asks Birbal to find the true merchant in the kingdom.  
- Birbal announces a test to identify the true merchant.  
- Merchants from across the land arrive at court claiming they have the desired item.  
- Some merchants bring silk cloth, others bring jewels and gems.  
- Birbal rejects all of them.  
- A simple-looking old man arrives at the court with nothing in his hands.  
- Akbar asks the old man what he wants to sell.  
- The old man says he wants to sell time.  
- All the merchants laugh at the old man's claim.  
- Akbar is surprised by the old man's answer.  
- Birbal explains that time is the true treasure, never lost and of great value when used properly.  
- Akbar understands Birbal's explanation and honors the old man.  
- Akbar declares the old man as the true merchant of the kingdom.
