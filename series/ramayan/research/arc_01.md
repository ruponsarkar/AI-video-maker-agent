## Bala Kanda – Valmiki Ramayana Story - Universal Teacher
https://universalteacher.com/1/bala-kanda/
- The Ramayana has seven kandas, with Bala Kanda being the first book of Valmiki Ramayana.  
- Bala Kanda narrates Prince Rama’s birth, youthful adventures, and marriage to Princess Sita.  
- King Dasharatha was the king of Ayodhya and had three wives: Kausalya (eldest), Kaikeyi (second), and Sumitra (third).  
- Kausalya was the daughter of the King of Kosala Kingdom; Kaikeyi belonged to the ruling family of the Kekaya clan.  
- Sumitra came from the ancient kingdom of Kashi.  
- Dasharatha had no children, which saddened him as he feared his dynasty would end with him.  
- Dasharatha visited his Guru Vashistha and was told he would have four sons.  
- Vashistha summoned Rishi Shringi to perform the Putra-Kam yagna (sacrificial fire for the birth of sons).  
- The gods gave Shringi a bowl of divine nectar.  
- As the queens ate their share of prasadam, an eagle snatched Sumitra’s share.  
- Kaikeyi and Kausalya each gave half of their share to Sumitra.  
- Kausalya bore Rama; Kaikeyi bore Bharata; Sumitra gave birth to twins Lakshman and Shatrughna.  
- Rama is portrayed as the seventh avatar of Vishnu, born into mortality to combat demon Ravan.  
- Bharata and Shatrughna are depicted as avatars of Vishnu’s disc and conch-shell.  
- Lakshmana is an avatar of Shesha, the nag associated with Vishnu.  
- Rishi Viswamitra trained the sons of King Dasharatha in missile-arrow techniques imbued with secret chants.  
- At 16 years old, Rama met Rishi Viswamitra, who was disturbed by rakshas Mericha and Subahu.  
- Viswamitra asked Dasharatha to send his sons to the forest, which he reluctantly agreed to.  
- Viswamitra took Rama and Lakshmana to his ashram to help slay Rakshas harassing him and other sages.  
- Rama encountered Tadaka, a cursed yaksha demoness.  
- Rama hesitated to kill Tadaka as she was female, but Viswamitra explained evil had no gender.  
- Killing Tadaka liberated the yaksha soul cursed for a sin.  
- Viswamitra gave Ram and Lakshmana divine weapons (astras and sastras) and taught them advanced military arts.  
- Viswamitra told Rama and Lakshmana to watch for Mericha and Subahu, who would try to defile a yagna.  
- On the seventh day of the yagna, Mericha and Subahu arrived with rakshas to disrupt it.  
- Ram killed Subahu with one arrow and Mericha with another; Lakshmana dealt with the rest of the rakshas.  
- The yagna was completed successfully.  
- A female child was found in a furrow in a ploughed field, considered a daughter of Bhumi (goddess of Earth).  
- The child was adopted by King Janaka of Mithila and named Janaki, later called Sita.  
- As a child, Sita unknowingly moved the table with the Shiv Dhanush (divine bow of Lord Shiva) during play.  
- King Janaka decided to use this incident as the basis for a swayamvar (self-choice wedding).  
- Rama went to Mithila for Sita’s swayamvar, accompanied by Viswamitra and Lakshmana.  
- They stopped at the hermitage of Rishi Gautam, where Viswamitra told Ram about Ahalya’s story.  
- Ahalya was cursed by her husband Rishi Gautam after being seduced by Indra, who took her husband’s form.  
- Rama freed Ahalya from her curse by touching the boulder she had turned into.  
- Ahalya thanked Rama and returned to her husband.

- Rama is chosen by the gods as Vishnu's incarnation.  
- Rama is described with divine attributes including lotus-red eyes, lengthy arms, roseate lips, and a voice like drumbeat.  
- Rama is born on the ninth day of Chaitra month in the twelfth lunar month.  
- The ruling star of the day is Aditi, and the ascendant is Punarvasu (Nakshatra).  
- Five of the nine planets are at their highest position: Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, and Venus.  
- Jupiter with Moon is ascendant in Cancer.  
- Rama's birth is celebrated as the Ramanavami festival.  
- Rama's early life includes being educated with Rishi Vasishtha.  
- Rama slays the demoness Tataka of the forest.  
- Rama slays the demon Subahu and his hordes who defile the yajnas of Vishvamitra.  
- Rama delivers Ahalya from her curse.  
- Rama marries Sita in Mithila.  
- Rama is crowned prince of Ayodhya.
