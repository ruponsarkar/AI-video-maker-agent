- Viswamithra tells the boys several more stories before they reach the site of the sacrifice.

- After the ceremony, Rama's brothers marry other women from Mithila, and they return to Ayodhya, the capital city of Kosala.

- Rama accepts this, and they begin to prepare for Rama's coronation the following day.

- Kaikeyi explains what happened and asks for Rama.

- Ministers, Lakshmana, and Kausalya attempt to convince Rama to stay, but he insists on leaving.
- Rama dresses in clothes made of bark.

- Sathrugna helps the three escape unnoticed that night.

## Story and Summary of the Ramayana - Holy Cross
https://college.holycross.edu/projects/himalayan_cultures/2006_plans/ptiernan/story.htm
- **Story Events:**  
  - The Ramayana is composed of seven books or kandas.  
  - Rama's boyhood and adolescence are described in the Bala Kanda.  
  - The court of Dasaratha and the events leading to Rama's exile are detailed in the Ayodhya Kanda.  
  - Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana live in the forest for fourteen years during Rama's exile as described in the Aranya Kanda.  
  - Sita is abducted by Ravana, the ten-headed ruler of Lanka.  
  - Rama resides in Kishkindhya with the monkey kingdom, seeks help to find Sita, and defeats Bali.  
  - Hanuman, a monkey general who can fly, is sent to find Sita and informs Rama of her location.  
  - Hanuman's tail is set on fire by Ravana’s men but he escapes and sets Lanka on fire.  
  - Rama, Lakshmana, and the monkey army build a causeway to Lanka.  
  - A cosmic battle ensues in Lanka between Rama and Ravana.  
  - Ravana is killed, Sita is freed, and she proves her purity.  
  - Rama, Sita, and Lakshmana return to Ayodhya.  
  - Bharata returns the crown to Rama.  

- **Characters:**  
  - Rama: The eldest son of Dasharatha, protagonist of the story.  
  - Sita: The wife of Rama, chosen by her during a swayamvara ceremony.  
  - Lakshmana: Rama's younger brother and companion.  
  - Dasharatha: King of Ayodhya and father of Rama.  
  - Kaikeyi: Queen and mother of Bharata, who demands Rama’s exile.  
  - Bharata: The son of Queen Kaikeyi, who is crowned king during Rama's exile.  
  - Ravana: Ten-headed ruler of Lanka, abducts Sita.  
  - Hanuman: General of the monkey kingdom, can fly due to his father being the wind.  

- **Places:**  
  - Ayodhya: The capital city of Dasharatha and where Rama is crowned king.  
  - Lanka: The island kingdom ruled by Ravana, where Sita is abducted.  
  - Kishkindhya: The empire of monkeys where Rama resides during his exile.  

- **Relationships:**  
  - Rama and Sita are married and deeply in love.  
  - Rama and Lakshmana are brothers and close companions.  
  - Dasharatha is the father of Rama, Bharata, and the twins.  
  - Kaikeyi is the mother of Bharata and the stepmother of Rama.  
  - Bharata is the brother of Rama and the son of Queen Kaikeyi.  
  - Ravana is the enemy of Rama and the abductor of Sita.  
  - Hanuman is a loyal follower and ally to Rama.

## The Ramayana – A Summary - Dandavats.com
https://www.dandavats.com/?p=85324
- The story of Rama is divided into four parts: Early life of Rama, Rama’s exile, Abduction of Sita, and Slaying of Ravana and Rama’s coronation.
- Dasharatha was the king of Kosala, an ancient kingdom located in present day Uttar Pradesh. Ayodhya was its capital.
- Dasharatha was childless and advised by his family priest Vashishtha to perform a fire sacrifice ceremony to seek children.
- Vishnu manifested himself as Rama, the eldest son of Dasharatha, to kill Ravana.
- During the fire worship ceremony, a majestic figure handed Dasharatha a bowl of rice pudding (payasa) to distribute to his queens.
- Kausalya gave birth to Rama, Kaikeyi to Bharata, and Sumitra to Lakshmana and Shatrughna.
- Rama’s birthday is celebrated as Ramanavami.
- The four princes grew up to be tall, strong, handsome, and brave. Rama was closest to Lakshmana and Bharata to Shatrughna.
- Sage Viswamitra came to Ayodhya and asked Dasharatha to send Rama to kill Rakshasas disturbing his fire sacrifice.
- Dasharatha agreed to send Rama and Lakshmana with Viswamitra after being assured of their safety.
- The party reached Dandaka forest where they faced Tadaka, a Rakshasi, who was defeated by Rama.
- Viswamitra taught Rama divine mantras to summon weapons for fighting evil.
- They proceeded to Mithila, the capital of Janaka’s kingdom, where Viswamitra wanted to marry Rama to Sita.
- King Janaka had a bow from Lord Siva that only the bravest could string.
- Rama easily strung the bow and it snapped in two, impressing everyone.
- Dasharatha consented to the marriage and came to Mithila with his retinue.
- Rama married Sita, Lakshmana married Urmila, Bharata married Mandavi, and Shatrughna married Shrutakirti.
- After the wedding, Viswamitra left for meditation and Dasharatha returned to Ayodhya.
- Rama and Sita lived happily in Ayodhya for twelve years.
- Dasharatha summoned ministers to crown Rama as prince of Ayodhya.
- Bharata and Shatrughna were away visiting their maternal grandfather.
- Kaikeyi was rejoicing with the other queens about Rama’s coronation.
- Manthara, Kaikeyi’s wicked maid, devised a plan to prevent Rama’s coronation.

- After defeating Ravana, Rama returned to Ayodhya, where he established his rule known as Ram Rajya.

- During his exile, Rama traveled across the Indian subcontinent with his wife Sita and brother Lakshmana.

- Rama won a battle against Ravana.
- Rama returned to Ayodhya with Sita and was crowned king amidst celebration and jubilation.
