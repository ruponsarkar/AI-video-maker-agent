# II. AYODHYA KANDA - KAISERI BOON

## Lakshmana - Wikipedia
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakshmana
- Kaikeyi demands two boons from Dasaratha: Rama's exile and Bharata's coronation.
- Though reluctant, Dasaratha grants them, leading to Rama's banishment.
- Dasaratha's granting of the boons leads to his death.
